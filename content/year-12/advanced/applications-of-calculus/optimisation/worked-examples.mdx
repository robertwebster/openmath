# Optimisation

## Example 1 — Maximising area with a fixed perimeter

**Problem:** A farmer uses 80 m of fencing to enclose a rectangular paddock against a river (the river forms one side; no fencing needed there). Find the dimensions that maximise the area.

**Step 1 — Set up the constraint.**

Let $x$ = length of each side perpendicular to the river. The side parallel to the river is $80 - 2x$.

**Step 2 — Write the objective function.**

$$A = x(80 - 2x) = 80x - 2x^2$$

**Step 3 — Differentiate and set equal to zero.**

$$A'(x) = 80 - 4x = 0 \implies x = 20$$

**Step 4 — Justify the maximum.**

$$A''(x) = -4 < 0 \implies \text{local maximum}$$

**Step 5 — State the answer.**

The perpendicular sides are $20$ m each; the parallel side is $80 - 40 = 40$ m.
Maximum area $= 20 \times 40 = 800$ m².

## Example 2 — Minimising material for a container

**Problem:** An open-top cylindrical tin must hold $500\pi$ cm³ of paint. Find the radius that minimises the amount of metal used (i.e. minimises the surface area $S = \pi r^2 + 2\pi r h$).

**Step 1 — Use the volume constraint to eliminate $h$.**

$$\pi r^2 h = 500\pi \implies h = \frac{500}{r^2}$$

**Step 2 — Write $S$ as a function of $r$ alone.**

$$S(r) = \pi r^2 + 2\pi r \cdot \frac{500}{r^2} = \pi r^2 + \frac{1000\pi}{r}$$

**Step 3 — Differentiate and solve.**

$$S'(r) = 2\pi r - \frac{1000\pi}{r^2}$$

Setting $S'(r) = 0$:

$$2\pi r = \frac{1000\pi}{r^2} \implies 2r^3 = 1000 \implies r^3 = 500 \implies r = \sqrt[3]{500} \approx 7.94 \text{ cm}$$

**Step 4 — Confirm minimum.**

$$S''(r) = 2\pi + \frac{2000\pi}{r^3} > 0 \implies \text{minimum confirmed}$$

The optimal radius is $r = \sqrt[3]{500} \approx 7.94$ cm, with height $h = \dfrac{500}{r^2} \approx 7.94$ cm — note that $h = r$ at the optimum.

## Example 3 — Optimisation from a word problem (box)

**Problem:** A 20 cm × 20 cm square sheet of cardboard has equal squares of side $x$ cm cut from each corner. The sides are folded up to form an open box. Find the value of $x$ that maximises the volume.

**Step 1 — Write the volume function.**

Base side $= 20 - 2x$, height $= x$:

$$V = x(20 - 2x)^2$$

**Step 2 — Expand.**

$(20 - 2x)^2 = 400 - 80x + 4x^2$

$$V = x(400 - 80x + 4x^2) = 400x - 80x^2 + 4x^3$$

**Step 3 — Differentiate and solve.**

$$V'(x) = 400 - 160x + 12x^2 = 4(3x^2 - 40x + 100) = 4(3x - 10)(x - 10)$$

Roots: $x = \dfrac{10}{3}$ or $x = 10$ (invalid since $x < 10$). So $x = \dfrac{10}{3}$ cm.

**Step 4 — Justify and state the answer.**

$$V''\!\left(\tfrac{10}{3}\right) = -160 + 24\!\left(\tfrac{10}{3}\right) = -160 + 80 = -80 < 0 \implies \text{maximum}$$

$$V\!\left(\tfrac{10}{3}\right) = \tfrac{10}{3}\left(20 - \tfrac{20}{3}\right)^2 = \tfrac{10}{3}\left(\tfrac{40}{3}\right)^2 = \tfrac{10}{3} \cdot \tfrac{1600}{9} = \tfrac{16000}{27} \approx 592.6 \text{ cm}^3$$

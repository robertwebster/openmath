# Annuities and superannuation

## Example 1 — Future value of an annuity using geometric series

**Problem:** Deposits of $2000 are made at the end of each year for 4 years into an account earning 5% per annum. Find the accumulated value at the end of 4 years.

**Step 1 — Write the future value of each deposit.**

Each deposit earns interest for a different number of years:

| Deposit | Made at end of year | Grows for | Future value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | Year 1 | 3 years | $2000(1.05)^3$ |
| 2nd | Year 2 | 2 years | $2000(1.05)^2$ |
| 3rd | Year 3 | 1 year | $2000(1.05)^1$ |
| 4th | Year 4 | 0 years | $2000$ |

**Step 2 — Recognise a geometric series.**

$$FV = 2000 + 2000(1.05) + 2000(1.05)^2 + 2000(1.05)^3$$

This is a geometric series with first term $a = 2000$, common ratio $r = 1.05$, and $n = 4$ terms.

$$FV = 2000 \cdot \frac{(1.05)^4 - 1}{1.05 - 1} = 2000 \times \frac{0.21551}{0.05} = 2000 \times 4.3101 = \$8620.25$$

**Using the formula directly:**
$$FV = M \cdot \frac{(1+r)^n - 1}{r} = 2000 \times \frac{(1.05)^4 - 1}{0.05} = \$8620.25$$

## Example 2 — Superannuation accumulation

**Problem:** Yuki contributes $5000 at the end of each year into a superannuation fund earning 6% per annum for 20 years. Find the total value of the fund at retirement.

**Step 1 — Identify the annuity parameters.**

$M = 5000$, $r = 0.06$, $n = 20$

**Step 2 — Apply the future value formula.**

$$FV = 5000 \times \frac{(1.06)^{20} - 1}{0.06}$$

**Step 3 — Evaluate.**

$(1.06)^{20} = 3.20714$

$$FV = 5000 \times \frac{3.20714 - 1}{0.06} = 5000 \times \frac{2.20714}{0.06} = 5000 \times 36.786 = \$183{,}928.00$$

## Example 3 — Present value and retirement drawdowns

**Problem:** On retirement, Yuki transfers her $183,928 into a new account earning 4% per annum and makes equal annual withdrawals for 15 years until the account is exhausted. Find the annual withdrawal.

**Step 1 — Set up the present value equation.**

The present value of the withdrawal annuity must equal the fund balance:

$$PV = M \cdot \frac{1 - (1+r)^{-n}}{r}$$

$$183{,}928 = M \times \frac{1 - (1.04)^{-15}}{0.04}$$

**Step 2 — Evaluate the annuity factor.**

$(1.04)^{-15} = \dfrac{1}{(1.04)^{15}} = \dfrac{1}{1.80094} = 0.55526$

$$\frac{1 - 0.55526}{0.04} = \frac{0.44474}{0.04} = 11.1184$$

**Step 3 — Solve for $M$.**

$$M = \frac{183{,}928}{11.1184} = \$16{,}543.16$$

Yuki can withdraw approximately $\$16{,}543$ per year for 15 years.

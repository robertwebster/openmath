# Applications and radial surveys — worked examples

## Example 1: Bearings and the cosine rule

From a lighthouse $L$, boat $A$ is 18 km away on a bearing of 045° and boat $B$ is 22 km away on a bearing of 105°. Find the distance between the two boats.

<WorkedExample>
  <Step>Find the angle at the lighthouse between $LA$ and $LB$.</Step>
  <Step>$$\angle ALB = 105° - 045° = 60°$$</Step>
  <Step>Apply the cosine rule in triangle $ALB$, with $LA = 18$ km, $LB = 22$ km and included angle $60°$.</Step>
  <Step>$$AB^2 = LA^2 + LB^2 - 2(LA)(LB)\cos(\angle ALB)$$</Step>
  <Step>$$AB^2 = 18^2 + 22^2 - 2(18)(22)\cos 60°$$</Step>
  <Step>$$AB^2 = 324 + 484 - 792 \times 0.5 = 808 - 396 = 412$$</Step>
  <Step>$$AB = \sqrt{412} \approx 20.30 \text{ km}$$</Step>
</WorkedExample>

## Example 2: Radial survey — area of a section

In a radial survey from central point $O$, two boundary posts are recorded: $OA = 50$ m on a bearing of 030° and $OB = 40$ m on a bearing of 095°. Find the area of triangle $OAB$.

<WorkedExample>
  <Step>Find the angle at $O$ between the two directions.</Step>
  <Step>$$\angle AOB = 095° - 030° = 65°$$</Step>
  <Step>Apply the triangle area formula with the two known sides and included angle.</Step>
  <Step>$$\text{Area} = \tfrac{1}{2}(OA)(OB)\sin(\angle AOB)$$</Step>
  <Step>$$\text{Area} = \tfrac{1}{2}(50)(40)\sin 65°$$</Step>
  <Step>$$\text{Area} = 1000 \times 0.9063 \approx 906 \text{ m}^2$$</Step>
</WorkedExample>

## Example 3: Two-leg journey — finding the interior angle

A drone flies 25 km from base $P$ on a bearing of 055° to point $A$. It then flies 30 km on a bearing of 135° to point $B$. Find the straight-line distance from $P$ to $B$.

<WorkedExample>
  <Step>Find the interior angle of the triangle at $A$. The drone flew to $A$ on bearing 055°, so the backward direction (from $A$ toward $P$) is $055° + 180° = 235°$.</Step>
  <Step>The forward direction from $A$ to $B$ is 135°. The interior angle at $A$ is the angle between these two directions:</Step>
  <Step>$$\angle PAB = 235° - 135° = 100°$$</Step>
  <Step>Apply the cosine rule in triangle $PAB$ with $PA = 25$ km, $AB = 30$ km and $\angle PAB = 100°$.</Step>
  <Step>$$PB^2 = 25^2 + 30^2 - 2(25)(30)\cos 100°$$</Step>
  <Step>$\cos 100° = -\cos 80° \approx -0.1736$, so the term $-2(25)(30)(-0.1736) = +260.4$.</Step>
  <Step>$$PB^2 = 625 + 900 + 260.4 = 1785.4$$</Step>
  <Step>$$PB = \sqrt{1785.4} \approx 42.25 \text{ km}$$</Step>
</WorkedExample>

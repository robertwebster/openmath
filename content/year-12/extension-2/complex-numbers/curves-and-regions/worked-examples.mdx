# Curves and Regions (Loci) — Worked Examples

## Example 1: Identifying a circle from a modulus condition

**Problem:** Describe the locus $|z - 3 + 2i| = 4$ and state its centre and radius.

**Solution:**

Rewrite the equation to match the standard form $|z - w| = r$:
$$|z - (3 - 2i)| = 4$$

This is a circle with:
- **Centre:** $w = 3 - 2i$, which corresponds to the point $(3, -2)$ in the Argand plane
- **Radius:** $r = 4$

Every point $z$ on this locus is exactly $4$ units from the fixed point $3 - 2i$.

## Example 2: Perpendicular bisector locus

**Problem:** Describe and find the Cartesian equation of the locus $|z - 1 - 3i| = |z + 5 + i|$.

**Solution:**

This says $z$ is equidistant from $A = 1 + 3i$ (point $(1, 3)$) and $B = -5 - i$ (point $(-5, -1)$). The locus is the **perpendicular bisector** of $AB$.

Let $z = x + iy$:
$$\sqrt{(x-1)^2 + (y-3)^2} = \sqrt{(x+5)^2 + (y+1)^2}$$

Squaring both sides:
$$(x-1)^2 + (y-3)^2 = (x+5)^2 + (y+1)^2$$
$$x^2 - 2x + 1 + y^2 - 6y + 9 = x^2 + 10x + 25 + y^2 + 2y + 1$$
$$-2x - 6y + 10 = 10x + 2y + 26$$
$$-12x - 8y = 16$$
$$3x + 2y = -4$$

The locus is the straight line $3x + 2y = -4$.

## Example 3: Region defined by a modulus inequality

**Problem:** Sketch and describe the region $|z - 2 + i| < 3$, and find the range of values of $|z|$.

**Solution:**

The boundary of the region is the circle $|z - 2 + i| = 3$:
- Centre: $2 - i$ (point $(2, -1)$)
- Radius: $3$

The inequality $|z - 2 + i| < 3$ represents all points **strictly inside** this circle (not including the boundary).

**Range of $|z|$:**

By the triangle inequality:
$$|z| \le |z - (2 - i)| + |2 - i| < 3 + \sqrt{4+1} = 3 + \sqrt{5}$$

Also:
$$|z| \ge |2 - i| - |z - (2-i)| > \sqrt{5} - 3$$

Since $\sqrt{5} \approx 2.24$, the lower bound is $\sqrt{5} - 3 \approx -0.76$. Because $|z| \ge 0$ always, the effective lower bound is $0$ (the origin $z = 0$ satisfies $|0 - 2 + i| = \sqrt{5} < 3$, so $z = 0$ is in the region).

The maximum value of $|z|$ approaches $3 + \sqrt{5} \approx 5.24$ but is not achieved (strict inequality).
